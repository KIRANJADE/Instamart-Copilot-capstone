Choosing K
----------
K=4 is selected for interpretability and the strong silhouette score. Inertia always decreases as K increases because each point can be closer to a cluster centre when more centres are available. Therefore, the lowest inertia alone is not a reason to choose K=8; the elbow, silhouette and useful business-sized segments must also be considered.
